A game idea.

I can't help but feel like
adding Terraria-like features to Celeste
would create an awesome open-world game.

![](/celesterraria.png)

Break everything with varied tools,
conquer enemies with powerful weapons,
build beautiful and expansive bases,
and explore the world with fluidity.
